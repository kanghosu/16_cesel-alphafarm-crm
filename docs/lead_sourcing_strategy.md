# Semi-Manual Lead Sourcing Strategy

## Priority

Use semi-manual sourcing instead of broad automated scraping. The goal is to collect fewer, better leads with official sources and clear revenue or investment readiness signals.

## Source Order

1. Naver Place and Google Business Profile for stores, cafes, bakeries, hotels, and spaces.
2. Official company contact pages for representative phone, inquiry email, or department channel.
3. Instagram public profiles only as a visual or DM supplement.
4. DART, Hometax, KOITA, SmartFarmKorea, KOTRA, Export Voucher, and Bizinfo for legal, facility, R&D, export, or support-program validation.

## Data Entry Rule

Only use contact information from official sites, official business profiles, official public portals, or official social profiles. Do not use blog comments, review author phone numbers, personal messenger IDs, or private-looking mobile numbers unless they appear on an official company profile.
